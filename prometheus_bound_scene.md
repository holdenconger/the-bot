PROMETHEUS BOUND: SHORT SCENE

DRAMATIS PERSONAE
PROMETHEUS, chained to a rock
CHORUS OF OCEANIDS
HERMES, messenger of Zeus

[Same cliff by the sea. PROMETHEUS is chained. The CHORUS watches.
The sky is gray.]

CHORUS:
    The wind feels strange. Someone is coming.

[HERMES enters, fast and serious.]

HERMES:
    Zeus says tell the secret name
    or things get worse.

PROMETHEUS:
    I will not say it.

CHORUS:
    Prometheus, please. We are scared.

PROMETHEUS:
    I will not help a bully.

HERMES:
    Then a dark pit, then the eagle again.

PROMETHEUS:
    I can take it.

[Thunder. HERMES leaves.]

CHORUS:
    Why stay silent?

PROMETHEUS:
    Because wrong should not win.

CHORUS:
    We will stay with you.

[Rain starts.]

PROMETHEUS:
    Let it rain.
