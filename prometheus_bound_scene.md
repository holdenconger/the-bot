PROMETHEUS BOUND: NEW SCENE

DRAMATIS PERSONAE
PROMETHEUS, a Titan, chained to a rock
CHORUS OF OCEANIDS
HERMES, messenger of Zeus

[The same cliff by the sea. PROMETHEUS is chained. The CHORUS floats
nearby. The wind is cold, and clouds are moving in.]

CHORUS:
    The wind is sharp. The sea is loud.
    Someone is coming. Who is it?

[HERMES enters quickly. He looks serious and does not stop.]

HERMES:
    Prometheus, I bring a message from Zeus.
    Tell him the name that could hurt his rule,
    or he will punish you even more.

PROMETHEUS:
    I heard you. I will not say it.

HERMES:
    Then you will be thrown into a dark pit
    where no one can hear you.

PROMETHEUS:
    Then throw me. I will not help him.

CHORUS:
    [Softly] Prometheus, is there any way
    to make this stop? We are afraid.

PROMETHEUS:
    I won't lie to make a bully happy.

HERMES:
    You call him a bully. He calls you a thief.
    You stole fire and gave it to humans.

PROMETHEUS:
    I did. I am not sorry. It helped them.
    I choose my own words.

[Thunder rumbles. The wind pushes the CHORUS around.]

CHORUS:
    The storm is starting. Tell us what to do.

PROMETHEUS:
    Be strong. Time changes things. Zeus is not forever.

HERMES:
    Time will not save you. The eagle will return.
    The rock will hurt you. You will break.

PROMETHEUS:
    It already hurts. I still won't tell.

HERMES:
    I warned you. Do not act surprised
    when the earth opens under you.

[HERMES leaves into the storm. The CHORUS settles down.]

CHORUS:
    He is gone, but his words stay. Why keep quiet
    if it could bring peace?

PROMETHEUS:
    His peace is just more chains. Someone must say no.
    Wrong things should not win.

CHORUS:
    Then we will stay and sing with you.
    We will not run away.

[Rain begins. PROMETHEUS looks up at it.]

PROMETHEUS:
    Let it rain. It cannot change what I chose.
